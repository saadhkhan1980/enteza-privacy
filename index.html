Trivenn Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the Trivenn iPhone app (beta)

In short: everything stays on your iPhone. Trivenn has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers and no analytics. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data.

What Trivenn stores, and where

To show your spending and which card to use, Trivenn keeps on your iPhone only:

  • Your purchases: merchant, amount, date and time, the card used, and the category you chose.
  • Where a purchase happened, when your own automation sends it (to name and classify the place).
  • The cards you told Trivenn you hold, and your answers to its questions.
  • The text of card alerts your automation passes to it, and statements you import yourself.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backup. Deleting the app deletes it.

How purchases reach Trivenn

You set up your own Shortcuts automations (for example "When a card is tapped" in Wallet, or notifications from the card apps you choose). They run Trivenn's actions on your iPhone. Trivenn never asks for your bank password and never connects to your bank.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Apple Maps. To find and name nearby businesses, Trivenn uses Apple's MapKit, which sends a location or a search phrase to Apple. Apple handles these requests under Apple's Privacy Policy. Trivenn doesn't receive or keep them anywhere else.
  • Only when you choose to share. If you export a CSV, share a screen, or use "Share diagnostics", the data goes where you send it. Diagnostics remove addresses, locations and card numbers first.

Nothing else leaves your phone. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no tracking.

Notifications and location

Trivenn asks for notifications to send a weekly summary and the occasional tip, and for location to match a tap to the place you paid and to answer "Which card here?". You can turn either off in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

Trivenn isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If Trivenn ever adds anything that changes this (for example optional sync through your own iCloud, or an opt-in way to share facts about public businesses), this page will say so before it happens, and it will be off unless you turn it on.

Trivenn isn't affiliated with any bank or card issuer. Card names are used only to identify your cards. Questions: during the beta, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app (or take a screenshot in Trivenn and tap Share Beta Feedback).
